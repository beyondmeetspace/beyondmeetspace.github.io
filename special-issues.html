Special issues

Members of the group are guest editors of two journal issues that grew out of the Meaningful XR conference.

Journal of Media Psychology

Metaverse-Mediated Communication: A Call for Theory-Driven XR Research

Published in 2025 and made with the Meaningful XR 2024 conference, the issue offers theory-driven examinations of how people interact in and with metaverse and immersive environments.

Guest editors: David Beyea, Vivian Hsueh Hua Chen, Rabindra Ratan, Maxwell Foxman, Alex P. Leith, and Brian Klebig.

Read the editorialSee the full issue at Hogrefe

Frontiers in Virtual Reality

Exploring Meaningful Extended Reality (XR) Experiences: Psychological, Educational, and Data-Driven Perspectives

The second issue extends the conversation from Meaningful XR to psychological, educational, and data-driven perspectives. The call for papers has closed, and invited papers were in peer review as of early 2026.

What we study

Five coordinated studies of virtual meetings, well-being, and equity, and the papers they produced, sorted into six areas.

Fatigue

Fatigue

Virtual meeting fatigue and well-being

How platform design, meeting structure, and individual attributes produce exhaustion in virtual work.

A meta-analysis of 38 studies identified feeling trapped as the strongest predictor of videoconferencing fatigue, r = .33 (Beyea et al., 2025).

  1. Zoom fatigue in review: A meta-analytical examination of videoconferencing fatigue's antecedents

    Beyea, D., Lim, C., Lover, A., Foxman, M., Ratan, R., & Leith, A. P. (2025). Computers in Human Behavior Reports, 17, 100571.

  2. Meeting needs: How social interaction anxiety, zoom fatigue, relatedness, and demographics predict virtual meeting feature preferences

    Lim, C., Leith, A. P., Ratan, R., & Bouzek, D. (2025). Computers in Human Behavior Reports, 18, 100673.

  3. Stress and coping in VRChat: A mixed-method case study of the use of VRChat as a coping tool during the COVID-19 pandemic

    Leith, A. P., Beyea, D., Jeong, D., Foxman, M., & Lim, C. (2025). Computers in Human Behavior Reports.

  4. Zoom fatigue and facial dissatisfaction relate to virtual meeting engagement differently in the US and South Korean contexts

    Lim, C., & Ratan, R. (2024). Cyberpsychology, Behavior, and Social Networking.

  5. Facial appearance dissatisfaction explains differences in Zoom fatigue

    Ratan, R., Miller, D. B., & Bailenson, J. N. (2022). Cyberpsychology, Behavior, and Social Networking, 25(2).

Avatars

Avatars

Impression management and avatars

How people present themselves through avatars, and what that means for professional identity.

  1. Examining attitudes about the virtual workplace: Associations between Zoom fatigue, impression management, and virtual meeting adoption intent

    Lim, C., Ratan, R., Foxman, M., Beyea, D., Jeong, D., & Leith, A. P. (2025). PLOS One.

  2. The hyperpersonal model of communication in virtual meetings: Exploring the roles of fatigue, social interaction anxiety, muting, and avatar use in meeting engagement

    Lim, C., Beyea, D., Leith, A., Foxman, M., Ratan, R., & Klebig, B. (2025). Proceedings of the Hawaii International Conference on System Sciences.

Presence

Social presence in virtual reality and virtual meetings

Equity

Equity

Gender, race, and virtual inequities

Where meeting design amplifies or reduces existing workplace inequities.

Women and people of color place significantly higher value on avatar customization in virtual workplaces (Lim et al., 2024).

  1. Openness to the Metaverse Workplace: Zoom fatigue and metaverse information seeking mediate gender inequities

    Lim, C., Ratan, R., Pandita, S., Foxman, M., Hales, G., Liu, H., Lei, Y., & Beyea, D. (2025). Human Behavior and Emerging Technologies.

  2. An avatar's worth: Valuation of avatar customization for the metaverse workplace is predicted by virtual meeting fatigue, gaming habits, and being a woman or person of color

    Lim, C., Ratan, R., Foxman, M., Meshi, D., Liu, H., Hales, G., & Lei, Y. (2024). Computers in Human Behavior.

Play

Play

Play and engagement

Playful design as a way to raise engagement and reduce fatigue.

In the two 2024 DiGRA papers, playful design elements such as gesture tools, stylized environments, and ambient virtual objects are associated with lower fatigue and higher engagement.

  1. Balancing fun and professionalism in game development: The dark and light side of play in virtual meetings

    Bouzek, D., Foxman, M., Lim, C., & Leith, A. P. (2025). Frontiers in Communication.

  2. Wanting playfulness to counter fatigue from virtual meetings: Associations with social interaction anxiety and workaholism

    Lim, C., Foxman, M., Leith, A. P., Meshi, D., & Ratan, R. (2024). 2024 Proceedings of DiGRA.

  3. Virtually limited: Boundaries of play in virtual reality production

    Foxman, M., Ratan, R., Bouzek, D., & Leith, A. P. (2023). Proceedings of the 2023 DiGRA International Conference.

Design

Design

Platform design

Design principles drawn from the studies and built into the platform in VRChat.

A synthesis paper in preparation, Beyond Skeuomorphic Virtual Meetings, draws the program into five design principles and introduces the platform as their implementation. A preprint is available on request.

  1. Making a virtual playground: Values-based game design in meeting platforms

    Foxman, M., Bouzek, D., Lim, C., Ratan, R., Klebig, B., Leith, A. P., & Beyea, D. (2024). 2024 Abstract Proceedings of DiGRA.

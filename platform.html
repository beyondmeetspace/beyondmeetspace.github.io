A meeting world in VRChat

A free prototype meeting platform, built in Unity and hosted in VRChat, that puts the design principles from our research into use.

Join the platform in VRChat

Works on a desktop or in a headset with a free VRChat account.

Five design principles

  1. 1

    Immersive platforms for social presence

    Immersive environments that support embodied interaction may improve social presence and emotional connection and may reduce fatigue.

  2. 2

    Avatars for impression management

    Stylized avatars let users control how they present themselves. Women and people of color place higher value on this customization (Lim et al., 2024).

  3. 3

    Flexible audio tools

    Users can form subgroups, broadcast to everyone, or keep spatial audio.

  4. 4

    Hybrid participation tools

    A physical room can stream into the world, co-located users are muted to each other to avoid feedback, and remote participants watch live.

  5. 5

    Playfulness for engagement

    Optional fidget objects, sketch pens, and expressive gestures, visible only to users who opt in.
